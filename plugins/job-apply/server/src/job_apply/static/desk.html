    Site passwords

    One password per job system: the desk makes your account at each of its employers and signs you in. It stays on this computer; Claude never sees it.

    How this works

    Each employer has its own account on its job site. Save one password for a job system and the desk creates your account at each of that system's employers (agreeing to its terms for you) and signs you in, on that system's own sites only; when a site turns the password down, it resets that account's password to this one through the site's "Forgot password" email (unless your profile says manage_accounts: false). It stays on this computer, in ~/.job-apply/secrets.yaml, and Claude never sees it. Use passwords you don't use anywhere else.

    Your email: with an app password for the address in your profile (for Gmail, make one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords; it needs 2-Step Verification), a job waiting on an emailed code or link gets it from your inbox: only mail from that job's site that arrived after it began waiting, read without marking anything read.
